# Properti dan konstruksi

Two kinds of site live here. A **developer or agent** sells homes or land;
a **builder, architect or interior studio** sells work. Read the parts that
apply.

## Who comes, and what they came to do

- Home buyer: a family checking a project they saw on a billboard or in an
  ad: the price and the instalment, the location and access, the unit
  types and their plans, the legal status, and whether it is real.
- Builder's client: someone planning to build or renovate: what this
  contractor or architect has built, how they work, a rough cost per
  square metre, how long it takes, and whether they serve the area.

Big money, long decisions, many visits, shared with family.

## What decides it

- Developer: unit types with land and building area, floor plans, the
  price and instalment simulation the developer publishes, the location
  with travel times to real places, the site plan, the legal status of the
  land and permits, the construction progress, and the developer's track
  record.
- Builder or studio: their own finished projects with photos and scope,
  the process and contract terms, the cost basis, the warranty.

## The pages

- Developer: Beranda, Tipe Unit (a page per type), Lokasi dan Akses,
  Fasilitas, Legalitas, Progres Pembangunan, Kontak.
- Builder or studio: Beranda, Proyek (a page per project with a real story),
  Layanan, Proses, Kontak.

## The first screen

- Developer: the project name, location, starting price, the main unit
  type, and the contact or "Jadwalkan kunjungan" action.
- Builder or studio: what they build or design, where, a real project
  photograph, and the consultation action.

## The main action

- Developer: a site visit or a brochure on WhatsApp, with a prefilled
  message naming the project and the unit type.
- Builder or studio: a consultation request with the project type, the
  location and the size.

## Rules of the trade

- **Selling houses before they are finished.** Landed and terraced houses
  may be marketed during construction, apartments before it, only once the
  developer holds land-use zoning certainty, the land title, the ownership
  status of the houses, the building permit (PBG) and a construction
  guarantee (PP 12/2021, Pasal 22B and 22C). The marketing must show,
  truthfully: the zoning letter number, the land certificate, the PBG number
  and date, the site plan, the specifications, the price and the promised
  infrastructure (Pasal 22D). Ask for these; without them, do not present
  units as on sale, and tell the user why.
- **No promised returns.** Never write a guaranteed yield, rental income or
  "investasi pasti untung" for a villa, condotel or plot: OJK's Satgas
  PASTI treats fixed or high guaranteed returns as a sign of illegal
  investment.
- Renders labelled as renders; prices, instalment figures and promotion
  end dates exactly as the developer gives them.
- Builders and studios: business licences, certifications and warranties
  only as held, projects only with the client's permission.

Checked November 2026 against the sources named; this is not legal advice.

## Photographs

Developer: the real site and progress, the show unit, renderings labelled
as renderings. Builder or studio: their own projects only, with the
client's permission; renders labelled as renders. A stock living room
passed off as a finished house is the fastest way to lose a buyer.

## Where the concept comes from

- The drawings: the floor plan, the section, the site plan, the specification
  list.
- The material: brick, timber, concrete, roof tile, from the project.
- The land and the access: the road, the station, the view, the slope.
- Construction as a sequence: foundation to handover.

## Clichés of this trade

- A smiling family in front of a rendered house with "Hunian impian".
- A drone shot at sunset and "Investasi masa depan".
- Gold and navy luxury for every project at every price.
- Countdown "promo terbatas" with no end date given.

## As a template

A fictional project or firm in a real growth area, with unit types or
projects that read true (areas, plans), a legal-status section with fields,
and progress or process. The buyer replaces: units, prices, plans, legal
facts, photos and renders. Design for 1 unit type and for 8, and for a
studio with three projects.
