# Travel dan wisata

## Who comes, and what they came to do

- Lodging: a guest planning a trip, often comparing with Traveloka, Booking
  or Airbnb, checking whether booking direct is cheaper, what the room
  really looks like, the location, and whether the dates are free.
- Tours and trips: someone wanting to know the itinerary, the price per
  person, what is included, the departure dates and the meeting point.
- Umrah: a family (often the children for their parents) checking the
  agency's licence, the package, the departure date, the hotel distance and
  the payment schedule. Trust is everything here; scams are common.

## What decides it

- Lodging: honest photos of every room type, the price per night and what
  is included, the location with travel times, house rules, check-in and
  check-out, the booking and cancellation terms.
- Tours: the day-by-day itinerary, what is and is not included, the group
  size, departure dates, the guide.
- Umrah: the licence number, the package details (airline, hotels and their
  distance, duration), the price and payment schedule, departure dates.

## The pages

- Lodging: Beranda, Kamar (a sub-page per type when each has its own photos
  and price), Lokasi, Kontak or Reservasi.
- Tours: Beranda, a page per trip with its itinerary, Jadwal, Kontak.
- Umrah: Beranda, Paket (a page per package), Legalitas, Jadwal
  Keberangkatan, Kontak.

## The first screen

What and where (the place's name and region), one honest photograph, and
the booking or availability action; for a trip, the next departure date.

## The main action

Ask for dates: WhatsApp with a prefilled message asking for check-in,
check-out and guests (or the trip and number of people). Trips with fixed
dates can be event products with a price and a date. Links to the OTA
listings as a secondary path when the owner wants them.

## Searches

- lodging type + place: `villa private pool batu`, `homestay dekat malioboro`,
  `glamping ciwidey`;
- trip + place: `open trip pulau komodo`, `paket tour bromo dari malang`;
- umrah: `paket umrah 2027 jakarta`, `travel umrah resmi bandung`, the
  agency's name with "izin".

## Rules of the trade

- **Umrah and special hajj.** Organising umrah for the public needs a PPIU
  licence, and special hajj a PIHK licence, now issued by the Kementerian
  Haji dan Umrah (UU 8/2019 as amended, most recently by UU 14/2025;
  Permen Haji dan Umrah 2/2026). Acting as a PPIU without a licence is a
  crime. Do not build an umrah sales site without the licence number from
  the owner: tell the user why. Show the number exactly as issued, and tell
  visitors they can check it in the SatuHaji app.
- Umrah packages: the price, what is included, the payment schedule and the
  departure date exactly as the agency gives them; no "pasti berangkat"
  promises the agency cannot back.
- Lodging and trips: photos of the real rooms and stops, prices and fees as
  they are, cancellation terms as the owner sets them.
- Licences ("berizin resmi", star ratings, awards) only as held.

## Photographs

Ask for: every room as it is, the view as it is, the bathroom, the
surroundings, the trip's real stops, past groups (with permission). The
most common complaint is "it looked different in the photos": no stock
pools, no borrowed sunsets.

## Where the concept comes from

- The place: its landscape, its light at a particular hour, its building
  materials, its distance from the city.
- The way there: the route, the road, the boat, the last turn.
- The stay's rhythm: arrival, morning, the day out, the evening.
- The trip's own documents: the boarding pass, the itinerary card, the
  room key.

## Clichés of this trade

- A full-screen drone video with "Discover paradise".
- Turquoise and sand colours for anything tropical.
- A booking widget that is really a WhatsApp link.
- Umrah: gold and green with a silhouette of the Kaaba on every section.

## As a template

A fictional lodging or trip in a real region with honest prices, rooms or
an itinerary, house rules, and booking terms. For umrah, the licence and
package facts are fields the buyer fills, with a note in the handoff that
they are required. The buyer replaces: every photo, prices, rules, dates,
legal facts. Design for 1 room type and for 10, and for a trip of 1 day and
of 12.
