# Event dan wedding

Two different sites live here. A **vendor** (WO, venue, decorator, MUA)
sells the same service many times; read the vendor parts. An **event** (a
seminar, a concert, an exhibition) happens once; read the event parts.

## Who comes, and what they came to do

- Vendor: a couple, or more often the couple's mother, comparing vendors for
  a date. They want to know if the date is free, what a package includes, the
  price, and what past weddings looked like. They decide together, over
  weeks, and share links in family groups.
- Event: someone deciding whether to come: what, when, where, who speaks or
  plays, how much, how to get a ticket, and whether it is still on.

## What decides it

- Vendor: the portfolio (their own events, a lot of them), packages with
  what is included and the price or "mulai dari", capacity for a venue, the
  area they serve, and how to check a date.
- Event: date, time with time zone, place with a map, the programme or
  line-up, the ticket categories and prices, and what is included.

## The pages

- Vendor: Beranda, Paket, Portofolio (galleries per event, with the couple's
  permission), Tentang, Kontak. A venue adds Denah or Kapasitas.
- Event: usually one page: hero with date and place, about, programme,
  speakers or line-up, tickets, location, FAQ, sponsors.

## The first screen

- Vendor: what they do, where, one real photograph from their own work, and
  "Cek tanggal" or the WhatsApp button.
- Event: the name, the date and time, the city or venue, and the ticket
  button; whether it is sold out when it is.

## The main action

- Vendor: a date check on WhatsApp with a prefilled message asking for the
  date, the city and the number of guests.
- Event: buying or registering. Event or webinar products sell tickets on
  the site (`kind: "event"` / `"webinar"`, with `kindConfig` dates); a form or
  an external ticketing link otherwise. A `countdown` only to the real date.

## Searches

- Vendor: `wedding organizer bogor`, `gedung pernikahan kapasitas 500
  bekasi`, `paket pernikahan 50 juta jakarta`, `mua pengantin sunda bandung`;
- Event: the event's name, `seminar ... 2027`, `konser ... jakarta`, said the
  way the organiser's audience says it.

## Rules of the trade

- Portfolio photos only from the vendor's own events, with the couple's
  permission; a styled shoot is labelled as one.
- Package prices exactly as given, with what is not included (pajak, service,
  transport out of town).
- Event: the date, place, line-up and prices only as confirmed; a speaker or
  performer named only when confirmed. If the event is moved or cancelled,
  the site says so first.

## Photographs

The portfolio is the whole case for a vendor; ask for the best 20 to 40
photos across several events, and the photographer's credit if they want it
shown. For an event: the venue, the speakers' own portraits, and photos
from a previous edition.

## Where the concept comes from

- The invitation itself: the card, the envelope, the seal, the RSVP.
- The day's order (susunan acara): akad, resepsi, the hour by hour.
- The venue's architecture or the region's ceremony, when the vendor works
  in one tradition (adat Jawa, Sunda, Minang), from the brief only.
- For an event: the ticket, the stage, the programme booklet, the badge.

## Clichés of this trade

- Script fonts and gold on blush pink for every wedding vendor.
- Floral corners and watercolour leaves.
- Two hands with rings as the hero.
- An event page with a dark gradient, a big countdown and "Don't miss it!".

## As a template

Vendor: a fictional vendor in a real city with three packages that add up
and a portfolio of several events. Event: a fictional event with a date a
few months ahead, a programme, two ticket categories as sample products. The
buyer replaces: the photos, the packages or programme, the dates and the
prices. Design for a portfolio of 6 photos and of 60, and for an event with
one speaker and with twelve.
