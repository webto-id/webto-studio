# Profesi dan jasa profesional

Several professions here are bound by a code of ethics that limits
promotion: notaries, PPAT and advocates above all. For them the site is an
office's information page, done with the craft of an excellent one: who,
where, which services, how to make an appointment. It does not sell.

## Who comes, and what they came to do

- Someone with a matter: buying land, setting up a PT, a dispute, a tax
  letter. They want to know whether this office handles it, in their area,
  what documents to bring, roughly what it costs, and how to make an
  appointment.
- Someone sent by a bank, a developer or another professional, checking the
  office's address and contact before calling.

## What decides it

- The practitioner's name, title and the area of their appointment or
  licence (a notary's work area, a PPAT's area).
- The services, described plainly with the documents usually needed.
- The office: address, hours, how to make an appointment.
- For consultants: their registration and the kinds of clients they serve.

## The pages

- Beranda, Layanan (with document checklists, which visitors use), Profil,
  Kontak. A page per service only when each has its own checklist and
  process.
- No testimonials, client lists or case results for the professions whose
  code forbids them (see below).

## The first screen

The name and title, the profession and its area, the address, the office
hours and the contact action. Calm, exact, unmistakable.

## The main action

An appointment: WhatsApp or phone to the office, or an enquiry form asking
what the matter is about. Never a price calculator or a "promo".

## Searches

- profession + place: `notaris jakarta selatan`, `ppat bekasi`, `pengacara
  perceraian bandung`, `konsultan pajak umkm surabaya`;
- the matter + place: `jasa pendirian pt tangerang`, `balik nama sertifikat
  tanah bogor`, `pembuatan akta jual beli`;
- the practitioner's name.

## Rules of the trade

- **Notaries.** The code of ethics (Kode Etik Notaris, Kongres Luar Biasa
  INI, Banten 2015, Pasal 4 angka 3) forbids publication or promotion of
  oneself with name and title in print or electronic media. It names no
  exception for a website, so whether an informational site is allowed is
  not settled. Before building, tell the user this plainly; the notary
  decides, and may ask their regional INI board. If they go ahead, the site
  is an office notice: name, title and area of office, address, hours,
  contact, the services as a list with document checklists. No
  advertising language, no testimonials, no client or bank logos, no
  sponsorship, no "promo".
- **PPAT.** The IPPAT code of ethics (ratified by Kepmen ATR/BPN
  112/KEP-4.1/IV/2017) also forbids self-advertising through publications.
  Treat a PPAT site exactly like a notary's.
- **Advocates.** The Kode Etik Advokat Indonesia (2002), Pasal 8, forbids
  advertising solely to attract attention and seeking publicity through the
  mass media. A factual site is commonly accepted; the Dewan Kehormatan
  decides in a dispute. Never promise a win or an outcome, and never list
  cases or clients without their consent.
- **Tax consultants, accountants, other professions.** Their associations
  have their own codes; keep the same restraint. Licence and registration
  numbers exactly as issued; no "pasti lolos pemeriksaan" or guaranteed
  outcomes.
- Superlatives ("terbaik", "nomor satu") are out for every profession here.

Checked November 2026 against the sources named; this is not legal advice.

## Photographs

Ask for: a portrait of the practitioner, the office front and the meeting
room. Gavels, scales of justice and stock handshakes say nothing about this
office.

## Where the concept comes from

- The documents of the profession: the deed, its paper and seal, the
  numbered minuta, the letterhead, the stamp duty.
- The order of a matter: from the first meeting to the signed document.
- The office's place: the street, the building, the city's land registry it
  works with.
- Restraint itself: a site whose craft is its precision.

## Clichés of this trade

- Gold on black or navy and gold; a gavel, the scales of justice, Lady
  Justice.
- Marble textures and serif capitals with wide tracking.
- "Solusi hukum terpercaya", "berpengalaman dan profesional".
- A wall of logos of banks and developers.

## As a template

with document checklists, office hours and an appointment action, written in
the restrained register the codes require. The buyer replaces: the name,
title, area of appointment, services, address and photos. Design for 3
services and for 20, and for an office with no photos at all.
