# Kesehatan

## Who comes, and what they came to do

- A patient, or the person caring for one, with a need now or soon: is there
  a doctor for this, when do they practise, how do I book, what will it
  cost, do they take BPJS or my insurance, where is it and is there
  parking.
- A returning patient looking for the schedule or the WhatsApp number.

Many are worried, some are old, most are on a phone. Calm, plain words and
one clear way to book serve them better than reassurance.

## What decides it

- The practitioners: name with title, specialty, practice days and hours.
- What is treated or done, in patients' words, with prices or "mulai dari"
  when the clinic publishes them.
- Booking: how, how far ahead, whether walk-ins are seen.
- BPJS and insurance accepted, stated plainly either way.
- Licences and accreditation exactly as held.
- The place: address, how to find the entrance, opening hours, emergency
  hours if any.

## The pages

- Beranda, Layanan (a sub-page per service only when each has its own
  explanation, preparation and price), Dokter or Tim with schedules,
  Reservasi or Kontak.
- A FAQ for preparation, payment and BPJS.
- Articles only when a practitioner writes or reviews them, with their name.

## The first screen

What kind of care, where, today's or the week's practice hours, and the
booking action. For a specialist: the specialist's name and specialty.

## The main action

Booking: WhatsApp with a prefilled message asking for the patient's name,
the complaint in a few words and the preferred day, or a reservation form
when the clinic answers forms fast. A phone call button for older
patients. Never a form that asks for medical history the clinic does not
need before the visit.

## Searches

- specialty + place: `dokter gigi anak tangerang`, `klinik fisioterapi
  bandung`, `psikolog anak jakarta selatan`;
- treatment + place: `cabut gigi bungsu di bekasi`, `tes darah lengkap
  depok`;
- BPJS and price: `dokter gigi bpjs bogor`, `biaya scaling gigi surabaya`;
- a doctor's own name with "jadwal praktik".

## Rules of the trade

Health service publicity is regulated (Permenkes 1787/MENKES/PER/XII/2010
on health service advertising and publicity, Pasal 5), and doctors are bound
by their code of ethics (KODEKI 2012, Pasal 3 and 4). On the site:

- No superlatives or "satu-satunya": not "terbaik", "termurah", "paling
  canggih", "nomor satu".
- No promise of a cure or a result, and nothing that raises hopes the
  treatment cannot guarantee ("dijamin sembuh", "100% berhasil").
- No patient testimonials or reviews about treatment, and no before-and-after
  presented as proof of results.
- No comparison with, or disparagement of, other clinics or doctors.
- No discounts, bonuses, gifts or referral rewards for health services;
  prices may be listed plainly.
- No service or method the facility is not licensed for, and no new method
  presented as proven when it is not.
- Practitioners appear with name, title, specialty, practice hours and their
  licence (SIP) number as facts, never as a sales line or a model in an
  advertisement.
- A doctor's own practice site stays informational: who, specialty, where,
  when, how to book. Nothing persuasive.

Health articles only when a named practitioner wrote or reviewed them.
Checked November 2026 against the sources named; this is not legal advice.

## Photographs

Ask for: the practitioners (a real portrait each), the waiting room and
treatment rooms, the entrance from the street. Never stock doctors or
before-and-after photos the clinic did not take with consent. No patient
faces without written consent.

## Where the concept comes from

- The instruments and documents of this care: the appointment card, the
  queue number, the prescription, the dental chart, the eye chart, the
  growth chart.
- The practitioner's own approach, when the brief describes one.
- The neighbourhood: the clinic that has served one street for years.
- Calm itself as a design brief: what a waiting room should feel like.

## Clichés of this trade

- Teal and white, a stethoscope icon, a heart-pulse line.
- A stock doctor with folded arms and a clipboard.
- "Pelayanan kesehatan terbaik dan terpercaya".
- Three cards "Dokter berpengalaman, Peralatan modern, Harga terjangkau".

## As a template

A fictional clinic with a clear specialty, two or three practitioners with
schedules (fictional names), services with preparation notes and prices,
a FAQ on BPJS and booking. No sample testimonials claiming results. The
buyer replaces: practitioners, schedules, services, prices, licences,
photos. Design for one practitioner and for twelve, for schedules that
differ per day, and for a clinic with no photos of its rooms.
