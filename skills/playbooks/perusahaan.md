# Perusahaan dan B2B

## Who comes, and what they came to do

- A purchasing officer or owner checking a supplier they heard of: does this
  company exist, is it big enough, does it make what we need, to which
  standard, can it deliver to us.
- A procurement team that needs documents: company profile PDF, product
  specifications, certificates, a legal name and address for a vendor form.
- A job seeker, sometimes. Point them to the right contact; do not let them
  crowd the buyer's path.

They come on a laptop at work as often as on a phone, and they read more than
a consumer does.

## What decides it

- What exactly they make or supply: product lines with specifications,
  capacities, minimum order quantities, lead times.
- Proof of existence and scale: the legal name, the address of the office
  and the factory or warehouse, photos of the real facility, years running,
  clients they may name.
- Standards: ISO, SNI, halal, export documentation, exactly as held.
- A named person or department to contact, and a fast answer.

## The pages

- Beranda, Tentang (the company, the facility, the legal facts), Produk or
  Layanan, and Kontak. A page per product line when each has specifications
  of its own.
- Klien or Proyek only with work they are allowed to show.
- A Karir page only if they really hire through the site.

## The first screen

What the company makes or supplies, for whom, and from where; the request
button ("Minta penawaran", "Hubungi sales"). A photograph of the real
facility or product, not a handshake.

## The main action

A quotation request: a form that asks for what sales needs (company, product,
quantity, destination), or a WhatsApp to the sales line. Offer the company
profile as a download only when the owner provides the file.

## Searches

- product + "supplier", "distributor", "pabrik", + place: `supplier kemasan
  plastik surabaya`, `pabrik roti tawar bekasi`;
- specification words buyers use: `pallet kayu ispm 15`, `besi wf 200`;
- the company name, often with "PT".

English pages matter when they export; plan them in `seo_plan.json` as their
own pages, not as a translation widget.

## Rules of the trade

- The legal name (PT/CV), NIB and certificates exactly as given, each
  certificate only when the company holds it now.
- Client names and logos only with permission; "dipercaya oleh" over logos
  the owner did not clear is a claim the company cannot back.
- Capacity and output figures only as the owner states them.

## Photographs

Ask for: the facility (production line, warehouse, fleet), the product close
up, the team at work, and certificates as scans if they want them shown.
Stock factories are spotted at once by people who work in factories.

## Where the concept comes from

- The product itself: its material, its cross-section, its packaging, its
  specification sheet.
- The process: raw material to finished goods, the line, the route to the
  port.
- The documents of the trade: the spec sheet, the delivery note, the
  container seal.
- The place: the industrial estate, the river, the port the goods leave
  from.

## Clichés of this trade

- A handshake, a globe, a skyline at dusk; "Your trusted partner".
- Blue and grey corporate gradients with a hexagon pattern.
- Visi, misi and "nilai-nilai perusahaan" as the second section.
- A wall of round statistics with no source.

## As a template

The fictional company makes one clear product line, in a real industrial
city, with specifications that read true. The buyer replaces: the products
and specifications, the facility photos, the legal facts, the certificates.
Design for 3 product lines and for 20, and for a company with one office
photo and no factory.
