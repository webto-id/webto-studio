# Komunitas dan organisasi

The interview table above is the closest fit for an organisation's facts
(legal name, management, legal status); skip its commercial rows.

## Who comes, and what they came to do

- A member or congregant looking for the schedule: prayer times and
  kajian, services, meetings, events.
- Someone who wants to join, volunteer or attend once.
- A donor deciding whether to give: is this organisation real, what does
  the money do, how do I give, will I see what happened to it.
- A partner or official checking the legal status.

## What decides it

- What the organisation does, concretely, and for whom.
- The schedule, kept current; an old date on the first screen tells
  visitors nobody runs the site.
- The people responsible: the board or takmir, with names and roles.
- For donations: the legal body, the purpose of each appeal, where the
  money goes, reports of past use, and the official account in the
  organisation's own name.

## The pages

- Beranda, Tentang (with legal status and the board), Kegiatan or Jadwal,
  Donasi when they raise funds, Kontak.
- News or reports through the blog when someone will post them.

## The first screen

Who they are in one line, where, the next event or the regular schedule,
and the one action (join, attend, donate).

## The main action

Join or attend (WhatsApp, a form), or donate. Donations through the
platform's donation products (`kind: "donation"` with a target), or the
official account details exactly as the organisation gives them. Show
progress only from real figures.

## Searches

- the organisation's name;
- kind + place: `masjid dengan kajian rutin di bekasi`, `komunitas lari
  bandung`, `panti asuhan di malang`;
- `donasi ...` with the cause, when they run a public campaign.

## Rules of the trade

- **Public fundraising needs a permit.** Collecting money or goods from the
  public needs a permit (UU 9/1961; Permensos 8/2021 as amended by
  Permensos 8/2024), issued by the Minister of Social Affairs, the governor
  or the bupati/wali kota depending on the reach. No permit is needed for
  zakat (rules below), collections inside a place of worship, emergencies
  within a limited circle, and mutual aid within a school, office, RW or
  village. An open donation page on the internet reaches the public: ask
  for the permit number and the legal body (yayasan or perkumpulan). Without
  them, tell the user before building it.
- **Zakat.** Only BAZNAS, a LAZ licensed by the Minister, or a UPZ may
  collect zakat; acting as an amil without a permit is an offence (UU
  23/2011, Pasal 18, 38 and 41). Mosque committees are exempt only where
  BAZNAS or a LAZ does not reach and after notifying the authority (Putusan
  MK 86/PUU-X/2012). A zakat page needs the LAZ permit or UPZ status from the
  owner; otherwise leave zakat out and tell the user why.
- Donation targets, totals and reports only from real figures; the account
  in the organisation's own name, exactly as given.
- Photos of children and beneficiaries only with consent, and never in a
  way that exploits their situation.

## Photographs

Ask for: the activities as they happen, the place, the people (with
permission; for children and beneficiaries, with care and consent, never
exploitative). Stock volunteers and stock children are a poor substitute
for one honest photo.

## Where the concept comes from

- The organisation's own material: the bulletin, the schedule board, the
  banner, the membership card, the annual report.
- The place it gathers: the hall, the field, the mosque's own architecture.
- The calendar: what happens every week, every month, every year.
- What the members make or do together.

## Clichés of this trade

- Two cupped hands holding a heart or a seedling.
- "Bersama kita bisa", "Mari berbagi kebaikan".
- Green and gold with a dome silhouette for every mosque.
- Progress bars with invented figures.

## As a template

A fictional organisation in a real city with a clear activity, a regular
schedule, a board and, if it raises funds, a donation product with an
honest target. The buyer replaces: the schedule, the people, the legal
facts, the account, the photos. Design for an organisation with one weekly
event and with thirty, and with no photos.
