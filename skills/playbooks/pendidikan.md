# Pendidikan dan kursus

## Who comes, and what they came to do

- A parent choosing for a child: is it safe, who teaches, what does it cost,
  how far is it, when does registration open. They compare three or four
  places and often decide with the other parent, so they share the link.
- An adult learner choosing for themself: what will I be able to do after,
  how long, how much, online or in person, which schedule fits my work.
- A current parent or student looking for the schedule, the holiday date or
  the payment account.

## What decides it

- The programmes with level, duration, schedule and the full cost (with
  registration, uniform or material fees named, not hidden).
- Who teaches: names, background, a photo. For a parent this is safety as
  much as quality.
- The place: photos of real rooms and play areas, the address, how children
  get there.
- Registration: when it opens and closes, the steps, the documents needed,
  the quota if there is one.
- Accreditation and permits exactly as held; results only as the owner
  states them.

## The pages

- Beranda, Program (a sub-page per programme only when each has its own
  schedule and fees), Pengajar or Tentang, Pendaftaran, Kontak.
- A school with news and events earns a blog; a small course does not.
- A FAQ for the questions parents ask on WhatsApp every week.

## The first screen

What is taught, to whom (age or level), where or "online", and the
registration action, with the registration period when one is open.

## The main action

Register or ask: a registration form when the institution processes forms,
otherwise WhatsApp with a prefilled message naming the programme. A real
deadline gets a `countdown`; never an invented one. A trial class ("kelas
percobaan") is a strong second action when the owner offers it.

## Searches

- level + kind + place: `tk islam di ciputat`, `bimbel sd depok`, `kursus
  bahasa inggris anak bandung`;
- skill + "kursus" or "pelatihan": `kursus menjahit jakarta timur`,
  `pelatihan digital marketing bersertifikat`;
- cost: `biaya masuk tk ...`, `harga kursus piano anak`;
- registration: `pendaftaran siswa baru 2027 ...`.

## Rules of the trade

- Accreditation, permits (izin operasional) and certificates exactly as held,
  with the issuing body; never "terakreditasi" without the grade and year
  the owner gives.
- Graduation, exam or job-placement results only as the owner states them,
  with the year; no "100% lulus" or "dijamin kerja" unless they can back it.
- Photos of children only with their parents' permission; prefer group
  shots or backs and hands.
- Testimonials from parents or alumni only with permission, verbatim.

## Photographs

Ask for: classrooms in use, the teachers, activities, the building from the
street, and work the students made. Stock children are a bad stand-in: a
parent looks for their child's future teacher and room. When there are no
photos, design with type and the students' own work.

## Where the concept comes from

- The material of learning: the exercise book, the report card (rapor), the
  timetable, the alphabet chart, the piano keys, the sewing pattern.
- The rhythm of a school year or a course: from registration to graduation.
- The skill itself: what a student can do at the end, shown.
- The place and the way children arrive at it.

## Clichés of this trade

- Primary colours, crayons and cartoon owls for every age.
- A graduation cap icon and "Mencetak generasi unggul".
- A row of happy stock children on white.
- Three icon cards "Guru berpengalaman, Kurikulum terbaik, Fasilitas lengkap".

## As a template

The fictional institution teaches one clear thing to one clear group in a
real city. Programmes with honest prices and schedules, a registration
process with dates, a FAQ. The buyer replaces: programmes, fees, teachers,
photos, the registration period. Design for 2 programmes and for 15, and for
an institution with no teacher photos.
