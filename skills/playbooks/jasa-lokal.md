# Jasa lokal

## Who comes, and what they came to do

- Someone with a problem now: the AC drips, the car will not start, the
  septic tank is full. They want to know if you serve their area, roughly
  what it costs, and how fast you can come. Then they call or chat.
- Someone comparing two or three providers they found on the map. They look
  for prices, proof of work, and whether a real person answers.

Most visits are on a phone, often in a hurry, sometimes from an older
person. Big type and one obvious button matter more here than anywhere.

## What decides it

- The area served, named by district or kecamatan, not "seluruh Indonesia".
- Prices, or "mulai dari" with what changes the price (AC 1/2 PK vs 1 PK,
  jarak, tingkat kesulitan). Visitors distrust a service with no number at
  all.
- How fast: same day, within two hours, by appointment.
- Proof: photos of their own work, before and after, the workshop, the team
  in uniform, the vehicle with the company name.
- Garansi: what it covers and for how long, exactly as the owner gives it.
- Opening hours, and whether they answer on Sunday or at night.

## The pages

- One page often does it: hero, services with prices, area, how it works,
  proof, FAQ, contact.
- Beranda plus Layanan when there are several services that each have their
  own price list and questions; a sub-page per service only when each has
  real content (see `skills/meta/seo.md` section 2).
- Never a page per kecamatan that only swaps the name.

## The first screen

At 390 px: what they fix or do, the area, and the call or WhatsApp button,
with the hours if they are unusual (24 jam, buka Minggu). A price "mulai
dari" here when the owner gave one.

## The main action

WhatsApp, with a prefilled message that asks for what the provider needs
(the address, the problem, a photo): `…?text=Halo%2C%20AC%20saya%20...`.
A phone call button next to it for people who would rather talk. A booking
form only when the owner reads forms quickly; an unanswered form loses the
job.

## Searches

- service + place: `servis ac bekasi timur`, `bengkel mobil matic depok`,
  `sedot wc tangerang selatan`;
- problem + place: `ac bocor air`, `mobil overheat`, said as people say it;
- price: `harga cuci ac 1 pk`, `biaya sedot wc`;
- "panggilan" or "ke rumah" when they come to the customer.

## Rules of the trade

- Garansi, certifications and brand authorisations ("bengkel resmi",
  "teknisi bersertifikat") only as the owner gives them; a brand name is used
  only when they really are authorised.
- Prices exactly as given; "mulai dari" with what is not included.
- Customer names and reviews only with the customer's permission.

## Photographs

Ask for: their own work (before and after on the same angle), the team at
work, the workshop or the vehicle, and the tools that set them apart. A
stock technician in a spotless uniform is worse than a real one in a dusty
one: visitors look for proof that this provider exists.

## Where the concept comes from

- The job sheet, the nota, the work order with its checkboxes.
- The tools and parts: the manifold gauge, the torque wrench, the label on
  a compressor.
- The route: the area map, the van, the time it takes to arrive.
- Before and after, the trade's own way of showing work.
- The workshop's sign and its colours, when the business already has them.

## Clichés of this trade

- A blue gradient with a smiling stock technician and a wrench icon.
- Three cards "Cepat, Murah, Terpercaya".
- "Solusi terbaik untuk kebutuhan Anda", "profesional dan berpengalaman".
- A counter of round numbers ("1000+ pelanggan puas") nobody gave you.

## As a template

The fictional business names its trade and its city. Services with real
looking prices and what changes them, an area list, a process of three or
four steps, a FAQ about garansi and payment. The buyer replaces: the
service list and prices, the area, the work photos, the phone. Design for
2 services and for 12, and for a buyer with no before-and-after photos.
