# Toko dan produk

## Who comes, and what they came to do

- Someone who saw a product on Instagram, TikTok or a marketplace and wants
  to see more of the brand, check it is real, and buy, often wondering
  whether it is cheaper or safer here than on Shopee.
- A returning buyer looking for a restock, a new colour, or the order
  status.
- A reseller or dropshipper looking for the partner terms.

## What decides it

- The products: clear photos, the price, the variants (size, colour,
  scent), what is in stock.
- Trust: who is behind the brand, where it ships from, how long, returns
  and exchanges, payment options, real reviews.
- For food and cosmetics: the registration numbers, ingredients and
  expiry, exactly as on the pack.
- How to buy: checkout on the site, WhatsApp, or the marketplace links, and
  why to buy here.

## The pages

- Beranda, Produk (the platform's product pages, `/product/<slug>`), Tentang,
  Cara Belanja (shipping, payment, returns), Kontak.
- A size guide for clothing; ingredients and usage for cosmetics and food.
- A blog when they write about use, care or recipes.

## The first screen

What they sell, the best product or the current collection photographed
well, and the buy action. A promotion only with its real end date.

## The main action

Buy. Real checkout through the dashboard's products when the owner ships
and wants payments on the site; `orderVia: "whatsapp"` for products they
sell by chat; marketplace links when that is where they want orders. One
primary path; the others secondary.

## Searches

- product + qualifier: `hijab voal motif`, `sambal roa botol`, `serum
  niacinamide untuk kulit berminyak` (product claims follow the rules
  below);
- brand name, and brand + product;
- product + place when they also sell from a shop: `toko kue kering lebaran
  medan`.

## Rules of the trade

- **Halal.** The halal label, and the word "halal" as a claim, only for
  products with a halal certificate from BPJPH (UU 33/2014 Pasal 25 and 38;
  PP 42/2024 as amended by PP 40/2026). "Tanpa babi" is not a halal claim
  and BPJPH warns that informal claims mislead; ask for the certificate.
- **Packaged food.** Home-industry packaged food shows its SPP-IRT (P-IRT)
  number exactly as issued (Peraturan BPOM 4/2024); food with a shelf life
  under seven days and ready-to-eat food are exempt. Food registered with
  BPOM shows its MD or ML number exactly as issued. Never invent or imply a
  number.
- **Cosmetics.** Only products with a BPOM notification may be promoted, and
  claims stay cosmetic: no treating or preventing disease, nothing that
  works like a drug ("mengatasi jerawat", "menghilangkan flek permanen")
  (Peraturan BPOM 3/2022; Peraturan BPOM 18/2024). No doctor or other health
  worker as an endorser.
- **Herbal products and supplements.** Registration number exactly as
  issued, and no claim that they cure or prevent a disease.
- "Original", "asli" and brand names only for goods the owner really sells
  as such; prices, stock and promotion end dates as they really are.

Checked November 2026 against the sources named; this is not legal advice.

## Photographs

Ask for: every product on a plain background and in use, the packaging,
the label (for food and cosmetics), and the people behind the brand if
they want to be seen. A product photo must show the product; stock is for
mood only.

## Where the concept comes from

- The product's own material: the fabric, the jar and its label, the
  ingredient, the craft technique.
- The making: the workshop, the kitchen, the dye bath, the hands.
- The packaging and the unboxing.
- Where it comes from: the region, the producer, from the brief only.

## Clichés of this trade

- A full-width slider of three banners with "SALE" and percentages.
- Pastel cards with a product floating on a gradient.
- "Kualitas premium harga terjangkau", "100% original".
- Countdown timers that restart on every visit.

## As a template

A fictional brand with 6 to 8 sample products in one clear category, with
variants, weights and honest prices, a shipping and returns FAQ, and the
label facts left as fields for the buyer. The buyer replaces: every
product, the brand story, the registration numbers, the photos. Design for
3 products and for 200, for products with and without variants, and for
long product names.
