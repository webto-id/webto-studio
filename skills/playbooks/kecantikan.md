# Kecantikan dan perawatan

A **klinik kecantikan** that offers medical treatments (injections, laser,
peeling by a doctor) also follows `kesehatan.md` for those treatments and
its rules. A salon, barbershop or spa follows this playbook alone.

## Who comes, and what they came to do

- Someone choosing where to get a cut, a colour, nails, a massage or a
  facial: what does it look like when done here, how much, how long, can I
  book Saturday afternoon.
- A regular checking the price list, the stylist's days, or a promo.

Mostly on a phone, often from Instagram or the map, often comparing with
the place across the street.

## What decides it

- The result: their own work, lots of it, recent.
- The price list with durations, clear about what changes the price (hair
  length, product brand).
- Who does it: stylists or therapists with names and what they are good at.
- Booking that works: hours, days, how far ahead, deposit if required.
- Cleanliness and the products used, shown rather than claimed.

## The pages

- Often one page: hero, services and prices, the work, the team, booking,
  location.
- Beranda plus Layanan and Harga when the menu is long; a page per
  treatment only when each has its own explanation and aftercare.

## The first screen

What they do, where, a real photo of their work or space, and "Booking"
with the hours.

## The main action

Booking on WhatsApp with a prefilled message asking for the service, the
day and the preferred stylist, or a booking link the owner already uses.
Service packages as products only when the owner wants vouchers paid on the
site.

## Searches

- service + place: `salon rambut keriting bintaro`, `barbershop dekat
  stasiun bogor`, `nail art jogja`, `pijat refleksi surabaya`;
- price: `harga smoothing rambut panjang`, `harga facial acne`;
- style words customers use: `potong rambut korea`, `balayage`.

## Rules of the trade

- Medical treatments (injections, laser, chemical peels, anything a doctor
  does) follow the rules in `kesehatan.md`: no superlatives, no promised
  results, no patient testimonials, no discounts as inducement.
- A salon or spa service is not described in medical terms: no "terapi"
  that cures, no "menghilangkan jerawat", "menyembuhkan", "detoks racun".
- Cosmetics sold or promoted on the site must hold a BPOM notification, and
  their claims stay cosmetic: no claim that the product treats or prevents
  disease or works like a drug ("mengatasi jerawat", "menghilangkan flek
  permanen") (Peraturan BPOM 3/2022 on cosmetic claims; Peraturan BPOM
  18/2024 on labelling, promotion and advertising). Show the notification
  number exactly as issued.
- Before-and-after only from the business's own clients, with their
  consent, same light and angle, and never for a medical treatment.

Checked November 2026 against the sources named; this is not legal advice.

## Photographs

Ask for: their own results (with the client's consent), the space, the
team, the products on the shelf. Stock models with perfect skin set an
expectation the salon will be blamed for. Before-and-after only with their
own photos, same light and angle, with consent.

## Where the concept comes from

- The tools and the station: the chair, the mirror, the comb in barbicide,
  the colour swatch book, the nail colour wheel.
- The price board on the wall, the appointment book.
- The time a visit takes, from wash to finish.
- The neighbourhood and the regulars.

## Clichés of this trade

- Blush pink, gold lines and a script font for everything feminine;
  black, brass and a pole for every barbershop.
- A stock face with flawless skin and a leaf.
- "Tampil cantik dan percaya diri", "glowing".
- Sparkle icons.

## As a template

A fictional salon or studio in a real city with a price list that adds up
(durations and what changes the price), a team of three with specialties,
and a booking FAQ. The buyer replaces: the work photos, the price list, the
team, the hours. Design for 6 services and for 60, and for a studio with
one person.
