# Studio kreatif dan agency

## Who comes, and what they came to do

- A business owner or a marketing manager looking for someone to make
  something: a brand, photos, a video, a website, social content. They want
  to see work like what they need, know roughly what it costs, and judge
  whether this studio gets them.
- A brand that already shortlisted the studio, checking it before a call.

The studio's own site is its first portfolio piece. Visitors judge it as
work, so the craft bar is higher here than in any other trade.

## What decides it

- Case studies: the client, the problem, what was made, the result the
  client may share. Fewer, deeper pieces beat a long grid.
- Work close to the visitor's own need, findable by type (branding, foto
  produk, video) or by industry.
- The people: who will do the work.
- Process and price signals: how a project runs, how long, "mulai dari" or
  packages when the studio publishes them.

## The pages

- Beranda, Karya (a page per case study when each has its own story),
  Layanan, Tentang or Tim, Kontak.
- A journal only when the studio writes.

## The first screen

What the studio makes, for whom, and real work: the work is the hero, not a
stock laptop. The contact or "Mulai proyek" action.

## The main action

A project brief: a form asking what they need, the budget range, the
deadline. Or WhatsApp to a named person. Booking a call works for larger
studios when the owner uses a calendar link.

## Searches

- service + place: `jasa foto produk jakarta`, `studio branding bandung`,
  `jasa video company profile surabaya`, `jasa pembuatan website umkm`;
- service + industry: `foto makanan untuk menu restoran`;
- the studio's name.

## Rules of the trade

- Client names, logos and work only with the client's permission; work made
  while employed elsewhere is credited as such.
- Results ("penjualan naik 300%") only as the client allows and the studio
  can back.
- Photos and footage they did not make stay out, however good.

## Photographs

The work, at its best resolution, and behind the scenes of making it. The
team at work. Never stock "creative office" photos.

## Where the concept comes from

- The studio's own working material: the moodboard, the contact sheet, the
  storyboard, the grid, the colour proof.
- The studio's method, when it has a named one.
- A signature from their own best work, extended to the site.
- The city they work from, when it shapes their work.

## Clichés of this trade

- Giant "We craft digital experiences" over a black screen with a custom
  cursor.
- Marquee of client logos without permission.
- Every section a horizontal scroll or a pinned reveal.
- Numbers "150+ projects, 98% happy clients".

## As a template

A fictional studio with a clear specialty, three case studies with a
problem, a process and a result, and honest service packages. The buyer
replaces: every piece of work, the team, the services. Design for 3 case
studies and for 24, for work that is all photos and for work that is all
video, and for a solo freelancer using it.
